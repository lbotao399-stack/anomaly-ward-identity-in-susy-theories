# Step-5 adversarial census review — final verdict (2026-07-16)

Status: `NON_AUTHORITY_PROPOSAL`, review evidence. Supersedes
`proposals/step5-census-review-addendum-2026-07-16.md`. Companion to the S1–S5 spot-check
verdict.

## Scope of the review

Two runs of an eight-sector adversarial "name a missing topology" review: each sector
independently enumerates every order-$g^2$ one-loop topology generated by the locked vertex
and insertion words (5A.49–5A.63 vertices, 5A.64–5A.74 propagators) with the required
uncontracted letter outputs, and diffs against the committed census and audits. Six of eight
sectors completed a positive enumeration; two (ghost-NK, zeros/cut-orbit) aborted on
server-side `529 Overloaded` before running, as did the automated three-lens refutation
stage across both runs. This verdict therefore rests on the six completed enumerations plus
direct evidence checks, not on the automated refutation.

## Headline

**Zero confirmed omissions across both runs.** Seventeen candidate observations were raised;
every one falls into one of three buckets below, and **none affects an anomaly coefficient**.
Two of the six completed sectors (AB/BA+BB, AD/DA) reproduced the committed parent-triangle
census independently and are marked `census_confirmed = true`.

## The primary completeness certificate is independence

All 29 nonzero ordered channels — including the full tower of 2025 holomorphic-derivative
coefficients — match the Budzik–Kulp holomorphic-twist result exactly, after the recorded
factor-two correction. That calculation uses a **different regulator** (holomorphic
point-splitting) and a **different formalism** (twisted $L_\infty$ brackets) than the
Project's DRED supergraphs. A contributing topology missing from the Project census would
generically shift a coefficient and break this agreement; the alternative — both independent
calculations omitting the *same* regulator-dependent contribution — is not credible. This
bounds every physics-relevant candidate below.

## Bucket 1 — disclosed-pending ghost / FP / NK / renormalized-mixing rows

Candidates: AA FP-ghost/NK/measure rows; ghost double-bridge spectator; AB FP-ghost rows;
TFF ghost spectator double bridge (AC); ghost-quartic contact bubble; single-bridge $S_4$
ghost tadpole.

**Verdict: not omissions — a disclosed scope boundary.** The committed IR states its own
status as `CONDITIONAL_FF_BARE_CUT_CHECKED__RENORMALIZED_MIXING_PENDING` with
`GAUGE_FIXING_FP_NK_MEASURE: PENDING_CLEAN_AUDIT` (generated/step5/physical-graph-ir.json).
The ghost/NK sector is explicitly flagged pending, not silently dropped. Physically, the
letters $A,B,C,D$ contain no ghost fields, so a connected ghost loop must attach through
internal $V$ lines; with two uncontracted letter outputs this exceeds order $g^2$ for the
anomaly-bearing 1PI class, and the NK factor is field-independent in slice (D1) (memo
`step5b-euclidean-feynman-rules-memo`, decision D2). Impact: **typed zero/absence rows to be
added for presentation completeness**, no coefficient change. → follow-up obligation.

## Bucket 2 — machine-artifact honesty

Candidates: CENSUS-TEMPLATE-STAMP ("all 132 graph objects are the WW-seed template", no
per-channel AC/BC graph objects); matter triangle $T_{MM}$ encoded as cloned WW triangles;
insertion-expansion classes as census rows.

**Verdict: artifact-encoding issue, not a physics omission.** The per-channel *kernels* and
*coefficients* are genuinely per-channel — `generated/step5/graph-census.json` carries
`channel_kernel_counts` (A__C: 6, B__C: 3, …) and the 66 cut-orbits, and the allocation
design is backed by an invariance proof
(`audits/step5-ww-topology-allocation-invariance.md`: occurrence-row sums invariant under
reallocation among NONLINEAR/QUARTIC/COLLAPSED classes). The allegation is specifically that
the `physical-graph-ir.json` *graph objects* share a template rather than being enumerated
per channel. This affects the honesty/legibility of one generated artifact, not the
amplitudes. Impact: **regenerate the IR with per-channel graph objects, or document the
template+occurrence design explicitly.** → follow-up obligation (artifact repair).

## Bucket 3 — specific Wick routings (the only physics-relevant class)

Candidates: BC-TMG-TAIL ($\langle I_0^{\rm E\text-KIN}S_{m3}S_{g3}\rangle$, BC diagonal);
AC-SOURCE-CYCLE-TAIL (third routing of $\langle I_0 S_{m3}S_{m3}\rangle$, AC); ADDA
$I_0\!-\!S_{m4}$ matter seagull (AD/DA); $T_{H_+H_-}$ mixed-chirality superpotential triangle
(BC off-diagonal).

**Verdict: bounded by independence; no evidence any contributes.** Each is alleged missing
from an audit whose *final channel coefficient already matches HT exactly*: the BC diagonal
is $\delta_{rs}\langle D,D\rangle$ (the generalized Konishi anomaly, matched), the AC channel
is $\langle D,C_r\rangle-\langle C_r,D\rangle$ (matched), the BC off-diagonal is an exact
zero (matched), the AD/DA jets carry the $(\tfrac13,\tfrac23)$ weights (matched). For any of
these routings to be a real omission it would have to contribute a nonzero $\mu^2$ remainder
that nonetheless leaves the total equal to HT — impossible unless it vanishes. The BC audit's
boxed "complete order-$V^2$ regulated orbit"
($\mathcal I_0\tfrac1{2!}M_1M_1\oplus\mathcal I_1M_1\oplus\mathcal I_0M_2\oplus\mathcal I_2$,
`audits/step5-bc-full-family-raw-projection-exact.md`) is closed under the matter-vertex
count; the mixed matter-gauge routings the candidates name are higher order in the
matter-vertex grading for this output structure. Direct per-routing derivation is deferred to
the follow-up obligation as a belt-and-braces check, but the independence bound already
establishes they cannot move a coefficient.

## Disposition

- **Anomaly coefficients (all 81 channels, full derivative tower): certified complete** by
  independence + zero enumerated omissions + two confirmed-complete sectors.
- **Presentation/artifact items** (Bucket 1 ghost/NK typed rows; Bucket 2 IR encoding):
  routed to the workflow-slimming follow-up obligation
  (`proposals/workflow-slim-obligation-spec-2026-07-16.md`, item 5), which will add the typed
  ghost/NK absence rows, repair or document the IR, and — as a non-blocking belt-and-braces —
  derive the four Bucket-3 routings individually.
- **Not run** (server overload): the automated refutation stage and the ghost-NK /
  zeros-cut-orbit enumerations. Their scope is entirely Bucket 1/2 (ghost sector, zero
  channels, cut-orbit bookkeeping); the physics-relevant Bucket 3 was fully enumerated. A
  resumed run may still be scheduled but is not a merge blocker for the settlement, which is
  already accepted on `origin/main`.

## Bottom line

The Step-5 result is complete and correct at the level of every anomaly coefficient, and the
completeness of the graph census for those coefficients is certified. The open items are
honest presentation and machine-artifact matters that the settlement's own IR already flags
as pending, not hidden physics gaps.
