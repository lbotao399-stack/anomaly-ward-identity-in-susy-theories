## 1. Why a second, independent route

The Step-5 settlement candidate (PR #61) computes the one-loop anomaly perturbatively:
DRED, an explicit sixteen-family graph census, and the evanescent cutting-rule failure
$\mu_\ell^2$ (verified in R.1–R.3 of
`proposals/step5-independent-physics-review-2026-07-16.md`). The review's single
largest residual risk is **census completeness** (R.6 item 3): that no same-order
contact, letter-expansion, ghost, or collapsed topology is missing from the per-channel
graph lists.

A heat-kernel-regulated Schwinger identity attacks exactly that risk, because it never
enumerates graphs:

- the regulator is built from the **complete second variation of the locked action**,
  so every same-order contact term, letter-expansion term, ghost loop, and seagull is
  automatically inside the regulated identity — a topology cannot be "missing";
- the identity is exact at the operator level (no expansion in couplings or in the
  background), and the anomaly is the finite $s\to0$ diagonal remainder — this is the
  precise sense of the owner's "non-perturbative";
- the arbitrary-holomorphic-derivative tower comes out of one Duhamel/simplex master
  formula instead of channel-by-channel tensor reduction; the derivation localizes the
  HT factor-2 source conflict (`HT-NORM-CONFLICT-ZERO-SHIFT-FACTOR-TWO`) to a countable
  insertion-pair multiplicity, whose per-channel count (Stage IV) completes an
  independent adjudication — independence of inputs, not of technique, since the
  universal integrals parallel HT's own Schwinger parametrization.

Agreement of the two routes (graph census vs. heat kernel), each derived only from the
locked action, is the strongest internal consistency instrument the project can have
short of the reviewed merge itself.

## 2. The physical design (fixed by this plan)

**(P1) Regulate the delta, not the graphs.** Every contact term produced by the local
Schwinger identity — project form (3D.34), specialized as in the Step-5 route — is
replaced by a heat-kernel-smeared kernel,
$\delta \rightsquigarrow \delta_s := e^{-s\,\mathcal K}\,\delta$, finite at coincident
points for $s>0$. No other element of the identity is modified.

**(P2) The regulator is the Euler-operator kernel.** $\mathcal K$ is the full
quadratic-fluctuation (second-variation) operator of the locked gauge-fixed Euclidean
action (4C.4) + (D1)/(D2) slice around an arbitrary background, organized as the
square of the first-order Euler system. It is a **matrix on the letter space** with
off-diagonal $\Phi\leftrightarrow\widetilde\Phi$ blocks generated by the superpotential
(4C.13) and $\mathcal V\leftrightarrow(\Phi,\widetilde\Phi)$ blocks generated by the
gauge–matter coupling. This is the owner's "运动方程里的算符" made exact.

**(P3) The naive regulator is provably wrong.** A per-letter diagonal choice such as
$e^{-s\,\bar{\boldsymbol\nabla}^2\boldsymbol\nabla^2/16}$ cannot reproduce the target:
it kills the $(B_r,B_s)\to\varepsilon_{rst}$ mixing channel that the HT target and the
Step-5 census both require to be nonzero, and it breaks the background-BRST covariance
that fixes all channels from one seed. The memo records this as an obstruction lemma,
not as a preference.

**(P4) Parallel columns.** Euclidean superspace is the primary column (matches the
Step-5 settlement scope). Lorentzian (proper-time $e^{is\mathcal K}$ with the locked
Lorentzian weight) and component-form (squared Dirac operator with Yukawa mixing,
Seeley–DeWitt $a_2$) are carried as parallel columns with the same numbered structure,
flagged non-authoritative where they touch the quarantined Lorentzian sector.

**(P5) External target discipline.** HT quantities enter only as comparison targets
(`external_target_only_claim_ids`); every coefficient is derived on the project side
first, then compared through the recorded dictionary.
