## 1. Canonical project boundary

The locked project contracts define two-component Lorentzian and Euclidean systems through Step 2B. They do not define a four-component Clifford package or component gauge-superfield normalization:

$$
\gamma_C^\mu,\ \gamma_{5C},\ \beta_C,\ \mathcal C_C,\ \Psi_C,
\ V_C,\ W_{C\alpha},\ [\,\cdot\,]_{F,C},\ [\,\cdot\,]_{D,C}
:=\mathrm{UNFIXED}.
\tag{D.1.1}
$$

Therefore no (W\leftrightarrow S) four-component, component-field, (F/D)-projection, or gauge-coupling map is automatically a project convention.

## 4. Project–Srednicki Lorentzian superspace

### 4.1 Coordinates and barred derivative basis

$$
\vartheta_C^a=\theta_S^a,
\qquad
\bar\vartheta_C^{\dot a}=\theta_S^{*\dot a}.
\tag{D.4.1}
$$

The project primitive is $\bar\vartheta_{\dot a}$, hence

$$
\partial^*_{S,\dot a}
:=\frac{\vec\partial}{\partial\theta_S^{*\dot a}}
=\frac{\vec\partial}{\partial\bar\vartheta_C^{\dot a}}
=-\bar\partial_{C,\dot a}.
\tag{D.4.2}
$$

### 4.2 Differential supercharges

Srednicki gives

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Q^S_a
&=\partial_a+i\sigma^\mu_{a\dot b}\bar\vartheta^{\dot b}\partial_\mu,\\
\mathcal Q^{S*}_{\dot a}
&=-\partial^*_{\dot a}-i\vartheta^b\sigma^\mu_{b\dot a}\partial_\mu\\
&=+\bar\partial_{\dot a}-i\vartheta^b\sigma^\mu_{b\dot a}\partial_\mu.
\end{aligned}
\tag{D.4.3}
$$

The project coordinate generators are therefore

$$
\boxed{
\mathsf Q^L_{C,a}=-i\mathcal Q^S_a,
\qquad
\bar{\mathsf Q}^{,L}_{C,\dot a}=-i\mathcal Q^{S*}_{\dot a}.}
\tag{D.4.4}
$$

Term by term,

$$
\begin{aligned}
-i\mathcal Q^S_a
&=-i\partial_a+\sigma^\mu_{a\dot b}\bar\vartheta^{\dot b}\partial_\mu
=\mathsf Q^L_{C,a},\\
-i\mathcal Q^{S*}_{\dot a}
&=-i\bar\partial_{\dot a}-\vartheta^b\sigma^\mu_{b\dot a}\partial_\mu
=\bar{\mathsf Q}^{,L}_{C,\dot a}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{D.4.5}
$$

Sources: Srednicki (95.11)–(95.12), snapshot lines 40–58; canonical (2A.4), (2A.21).

The phase in (D.4.4) belongs to the coordinate representation. It is not a phase redefinition of the Noether charge:

$$
[Q^L_{C,a},\Phi\}=+i\mathcal Q^S_a\Phi,
\qquad
Q^L_{C,a}=Q^S_a.
\tag{D.4.6}
$$

Here Srednicki's ([\Phi,Q_a]=-i\mathcal Q_a\Phi) is (95.8), snapshot lines 31–40; project diagonal covariance is (2A.16)–(2A.19).
