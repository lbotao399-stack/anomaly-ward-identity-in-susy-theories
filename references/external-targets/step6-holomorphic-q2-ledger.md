# Step 6 external target ledger

## 1. Published selection rule

Source: Budzik–Gaiotto–Kulp–Williams–Wu–Yu, *Semi-Chiral Operators in 4d N=1 Gauge Theories*, arXiv:2306.01039v2, Eq. (3.43), PDF p. 26.

$$
Q_nV(N_b,N_c,J_1,J_2)=0,
\qquad
N_b+N_c<n+1.
$$

Hence, for $n=2$, $N_b=2$, $N_c=0$,

$$
2<3,
$$

$$
\boxed{Q_2(b^Ab^B)=0.}
$$

This equality is the exact consequence of Eq. (3.43); it is not printed there as a separate open-index formula.

Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01039

## 2. Published universal bitriangle master

Source: Budzik–Gaiotto–Kulp–Wu–Yu, *Feynman Diagrams in Four-Dimensional Holomorphic Theories and the Operatope*, arXiv:2207.14321v1, Sec. 4.5–4.6, Eq. (4.44), PDF p. 29.

$$
f[\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3;0,0]
=
\frac1{24}
\bigl(\lambda_1\wedge(\lambda_2+2\lambda_3)\bigr)
\bigl(\lambda_2\wedge(\lambda_1+2\lambda_3)\bigr).
$$

This is a universal holomorphic $K_4\setminus e$ integral, not the operator equation $Q_2(b^Ab^B)$.

Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14321

## 3. Nonzero operator example, for disambiguation

Source: arXiv:2306.01039v2, Eq. (C.8), PDF p. 61.

$$
Q_2\!\left(b_Ab^Ab_Bb^B\right)
=
\frac{20C_1^2}{3}\epsilon_{ABC}
b^A\partial_\alpha\partial_\beta b^B
\partial^\alpha\partial^\beta c^C.
$$

The input has four $b$ letters; it is not $Q_2(b^Ab^B)$.
